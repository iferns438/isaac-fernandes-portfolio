HOW TO VIEW
1. Upload the contents of this folder to your web host, keeping the folder structure intact.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Keep the videos and thumbnails folders beside index.html so all media works.

PORTFOLIO NOTES
This portfolio presents selected campaign content and creative contributions. Depending on the project, work included planning, filming, editing in VN Video Editor, captions, hashtags, scheduling and publishing. Brand names identify the featured campaigns. The Morning vs. Night Owl video is presented separately as a personal on-camera appearance.
